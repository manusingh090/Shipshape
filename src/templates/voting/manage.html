Why quadratic, with a ceiling

In simulated votes with a group of friends all backing one weak project, plain one-person-one-vote let that project into the top three more than half the time once the group was a tenth of the voters. Quadratic voting with a 3-vote ceiling kept it out every time. The numbers, and where it still fails (people voting many times), are in JUDGING.md, section 10.
