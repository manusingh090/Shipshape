What comes in

From fixtures.json: the event and deadline, tracks, judges and their tracks, teams and members, projects and scores.

From a Shipshape archive, also: every detail and date, prizes and winners, form questions and answers, tags, links and images, the rubric's weights, assignments and score history, conflicts, the community vote and its ballots, comments and the activity log.

Never: passwords (people are matched by email; new ones get an account with no password yet) and certificates, which stay valid with the key that signed them.

The portal's rules still apply: staff don't compete, a second project from one team is kept as a flagged duplicate, anything handed in late arrives as a draft. Each exception is listed in the preview.
